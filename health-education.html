Know When to Seek Care

Recognise warning signs and understand why timely assessment by a qualified health professional matters.

Learn more →
01 · Maternal Health

Protecting Mothers and Babies

Regular antenatal visits help monitor the health of a pregnant woman and her baby. Seek urgent medical attention for heavy bleeding, severe headache, blurred vision, convulsions, difficulty breathing or severe abdominal pain during pregnancy.

02 · Nutrition

Eat Well, Live Well

A balanced diet includes vegetables, fruits, proteins, whole grains and healthy fats. Drink safe water and practise proper food hygiene. Children, pregnant women and people with special nutritional needs may require individual advice from a qualified health professional.

03 · Malaria Prevention

Prevent Malaria, Protect Your Family

Sleep under an insecticide-treated mosquito net, remove standing water around living areas and follow local malaria prevention guidance. Fever can have several causes. Seek testing promptly and take prescribed treatment as directed.

04 · Hygiene & Sanitation

Clean Habits Save Lives

Wash your hands with soap and safe water before preparing food, before eating, after using the toilet and after cleaning a child. Store drinking water safely and dispose of waste properly.

05 · Disease Prevention

Prevention Starts with Awareness

Keep vaccinations up to date, practise respiratory hygiene, avoid sharing personal items that may spread infection and seek medical advice when symptoms develop. Follow guidance from qualified healthcare professionals.

06 · Early Care

Know When to Seek Medical Attention

Difficulty breathing, chest pain, unconsciousness, severe bleeding, seizures and signs of stroke require emergency medical attention. Do not delay seeking help when a person is seriously ill.
