ABOUT MEDITRUST

Putting People at the Heart of Healthcare

MediTrust Health Centre is a community-focused healthcare facility in Kakuma, Turkana County, Kenya, dedicated to promoting compassionate, accessible and patient-centred healthcare.

Through health education, disease prevention awareness and community engagement, we strive to empower individuals and families with knowledge that supports informed health decisions and better wellbeing.
