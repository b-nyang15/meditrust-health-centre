ABOUT MEDITRUST

Healthcare Built Around People

MediTrust Health Centre is a community-focused healthcare facility in Kakuma, Turkana County, Kenya, committed to providing compassionate, accessible and patient-centred healthcare.

Through health education, preventive care information and a commitment to dignity, we aim to support healthier individuals, families and communities.
