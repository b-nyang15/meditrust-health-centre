Our Healthcare Team

Discover the professional roles that contribute to compassionate, patient-centred healthcare, health education and stronger community wellbeing at MediTrust Health Centre.

Professional Care. Human Compassion.

Working Together for Better Health

Effective healthcare depends on collaboration between different professionals. Each role contributes to patient support, health education, disease prevention and the wellbeing of our community.

MD
Medical Leadership

Medical Officer

Provides clinical leadership, assesses patients, supports diagnosis and guides appropriate treatment in accordance with professional standards and scope of practice.

RN
Nursing Department

Registered Nurse

Provides nursing care through patient assessments, health monitoring, treatment support, health education and compassionate assistance throughout the care process.

MW
Maternal & Child Health

Midwife

Supports maternal and newborn wellbeing through pregnancy education, antenatal and postnatal care, birth preparedness and appropriate maternity support.

CH
Community Health

Community Health Promoter

Promotes community health awareness, disease prevention, hygiene education and community engagement while connecting individuals and families to appropriate healthcare services.

NU
Nutrition Department

Nutritionist

Supports healthy living through nutrition education, dietary guidance, nutritional assessments and awareness of balanced diets and healthy eating habits.

AD
Administration

Healthcare Administrator

Coordinates administrative activities, supports communication, maintains organisational processes and helps ensure the smooth operation of the healthcare facility.
