Working Together for Better Health

Our healthcare team brings together different professional disciplines to support individuals, families and the wider community through compassionate, patient-centred care.

MD
Medical Leadership

Medical Officer

Responsible for providing clinical leadership, patient assessment, diagnosis and appropriate treatment within their professional scope.

RN
Nursing Department

Registered Nurse

Responsible for supporting patient care, nursing assessments, health monitoring, treatment support and patient education.

MW
Maternal & Child Health

Midwife

Responsible for supporting maternal health, pregnancy education, newborn care and appropriate maternity services.

CH
Community Health

Community Health Promoter

Responsible for promoting health awareness, disease prevention, community engagement and links to appropriate healthcare.

NU
Nutrition Department

Nutritionist

Responsible for supporting nutrition education, dietary assessment and healthy eating awareness.

AD
Administration

Healthcare Administrator

Responsible for coordinating administrative processes, communication and efficient facility operations.
